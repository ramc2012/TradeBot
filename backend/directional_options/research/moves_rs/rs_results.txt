1. RS FORMULATIONS AND HOW MUCH THEY OVERLAP
==============================================================================
computed:
  rs_ret_21    log(P/N) 21-session relative return   [PRIMARY]
  rs_ret_63    same, 63 sessions
  rs_slope_21  normalised OLS slope of log(P/N), 21 sessions
  alpha_21     21-session return minus beta_120 x NIFTY return
  beta_120     the control
  *_rank       cross-sectional percentile of each, per session

PRIMARY = rs_ret_21. Reason: the lane chooses among instruments on a
single day, so the natural object is a same-day cross-sectional
comparison; 21 sessions matches the monthly frame the owner asked
about; and it is the plainest reading of 'relative strength vs NIFTY'.

IMPORTANT: a per-date Spearman IC is rank-invariant, so rs_ret_21 and
its cross-sectional percentile rs_ret_21_rank have IDENTICAL IC by
construction. The rank form is a distinct object only for decile and
pooled work, never for IC. Reporting them as two 'formulations' with
two ICs would be double counting, and is not done here.
