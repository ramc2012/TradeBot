A8. WHY IS ATR-NORMALISED PERSISTENCE NEGATIVE? -- the mechanical check
====================================================================================================
  rank corr(this month's move count, NEXT month's ADR / this month's ADR) = -0.122
  => a movey month INFLATES the stock's own ATR, raising next month's K x ATR bar.
     That feedback is mechanical, and it is why the ATR-normalised persistence is
     negative. The fixed-%% definition (A7) has no such feedback and shows ~ZERO
     persistence. Neither formulation gives a usable positive signal.
